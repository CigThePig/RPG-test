# Free-form melee and dodge

The player can swing the sword into empty space. The swing locks the current PPC yaw at initiation; the trail, collision-safe lunge, and impact query all use that same ground-space direction. At the existing windup boundary the world selects the nearest eligible hostile within the 110-degree arc and current reach, with ordinal actor IDs as the tie-break. A miss still consumes the ordinary cost and cooldown. NPC attacks, projectiles, healing, and other SPELL abilities keep their targeted execution path.

Soft aim assist is evaluated once, when the player initiates a sword swing. It considers only visible eligible enemies within a 65-degree forward half-cone and within the strike reach plus the lunge and impact tolerance. A deterministic angle/distance preference selects one candidate. The signed facing correction is capped at 15 degrees with distance/angle falloff. There is no target snapping, guaranteed hit, chase, aim correction after launch, change to NPC combat, or change to the authoritative 110-degree impact arc. The corrected initial direction is shared by the existing sword trail, collision-aware lunge and impact query. When no candidate qualifies, free-form attacks are identical to the prior implementation.

The 12-unit lunge is world movement over 0.08–0.18 seconds and is clipped by existing terrain/door collision. The existing windup, recovery, lunge and trail remain authoritative or presentation-aligned; the biped weapon now explicitly requests PPC's timed `Strike` pose.

Enemy bite windups show rings around the attacker and its designated target, connected by a dashed tracking line. The mark follows the target: the bite still checks the target's actual range, line of sight and defenses at impact. Moving away, using terrain, interrupting the enemy, or dodging during the established immunity interval can prevent damage. The warning ends on release or interruption.

The verification-only `fixture_sweep` demonstrates a different rule: its outlined ground sector is a directional cone committed when the attack starts. Moving outside that sector before impact avoids it; moving the target does not rotate it. Both shapes are projected from ground space onto the same camera plane as the creatures. Neither warning controls damage or adds windup time.

Dodge has its own action state and 0.70-second initiation cooldown. It covers up to 90 ground units over 0.40 seconds with ease-out, subject to collision. The direction is the current unsmoothed movement intent, or visual facing if neutral, and is locked for the roll. Direct hits are rejected from 0.10 seconds through just before 0.30 seconds; existing periodic damage retains its normal rules. An early or late hit interrupts the roll. Opening a modal, backgrounding, or loading a saved roll clears its transient motion but retains a valid cooldown. Space dodges on desktop; the separate mobile button is beside Attack. Release inside the original button to activate it.

`game/combat/CombatTuning.cs` contains the new arc, lunge, and dodge constants; existing slash timing, base range, cost, cooldown, and damage remain in `game/data/foundation.json`. Dodge presentation uses the authoritative action progress, with a biped tuck and rotation and a limited fallback for other families. It does not drive world position or damage.

The automated replay and Android emulator exercise controls and state, but they cannot establish subjective combat feel or phone ergonomics. Physical Galaxy S25 playtesting remains necessary. Development APKs may use an ephemeral signing key; a differently signed installed build may need to be uninstalled first, which can remove local saves.

## Contact-aware roll presentation

Biped rolling is now a full-body PPC action, not an additive 360-degree torso spin. `RollMotion.cs` produces an unwrapped, travel-relative rotation and phased crouch/tuck/landing sequence. `RigDriver` accepts an explicit action quaternion and a two-pass skeletal support adjustment for head/chest/shoulder clearance. `LeggedLocomotion` suspends walking feet and solves action-specific arm/leg targets before restoring world-space plants on exit. The character remains at the authoritative ground position; no animation step grants movement or immunity. Non-bipeds retain a non-spinning crouch. The player-only `RollDustOverlay` now emits substantial, irregular takeoff and trailing clouds at world-anchored ground positions, then a larger landing cloud that persists after the 0.4-second dodge ends. Each puff grows, drifts and fades independently over ~0.5–0.66 seconds. The palette responds to grass, earth, floor, rock and water, with enough size and contrast to read at ordinary phone zoom rather than as a texture glitch. The emitter has a bounded 64-puff budget, deterministic placement, no texture assets, and no physics, status, movement, immunity, save data or random-state effects. Interruption prevents a false landing burst; teleport resets particles. The native visual verification captures takeoff, travel, landing, and lingering frames at normal gameplay zoom for review. Player roll and active combat poses target 30 FPS while idle and locomotion retain their preset sampling rates. This is an engineering prototype pending physical S25 visual acceptance; the simplified bone-volume contact approximation can still require tuning for extreme generated bodies and near walls.

## Combat feedback

Bites and the verification cone use their authored PPC action styles. Successful damage produces a directional recoil and contact flash; a miss produces a lighter whiff, and a successful dodge has a separate cool-colored ring. Defeat transitions into the creature rig's rest posture while the loot marker remains available. Projectile launch and actual collision have separate visual cues. These are observers of world decisions and do not grant movement, damage, immunity or extra stun. Camera feedback is a small bounded presentation offset, configurable through `CameraFeedback.Strength`; setting it to zero disables the nudge.

## Tactical enemies (Part 3)

Ruin Prowlers pressure close range with bites and occasional lateral movement. Brutes close slowly and commit a readable Slam cone, then recover. Spitters preserve moderate range and release straight droplets; movement can evade their actual flight. NPC avoidance gently separates living bodies without making them solid obstacles for the player. The approved sword, aim assist, lunge, movement and roll constants are unchanged.

Developer diagnostics provides a reversible **Combat archetypes test encounter (F9)**. Normal progress is preserved by the existing feel-course snapshot; return through the feel lab. This developer shortcut is separate from player controls. Part 4 owns layout and final balance.

## Part 4 mobile layout

`MobileCombatControls` owns versioned geometry and registered controls. `RpgUi.ActionControl(id)` exposes attack, dodge, ember, mend, potion, interact, inventory and menu independently of labels/cooldown text. Attack (116×112 logical units) is at the lower right; Dodge (108×104) is immediately inward and above. The three secondary abilities use a separate 100×72 row. Interact has a fixed inward position; Pack/Menu occupy the upper right. The joystick remains left. No gesture shortcuts were introduced.

Android display safe pixels are converted with the inverse viewport screen transform, clipped to the logical viewport, then used for edge margins. This includes canvas_items stretching and letterboxing rather than comparing pixels to Control coordinates. Layout runs on viewport resize; a proportional compact fallback applies below a 900×560 usable region. Physical cutout/gesture behaviour still requires phone acceptance. Primary hitboxes do not resize with cooldown labels. Interact stays discoverable; an existing production query brightens it at 10 Hz with a 350 ms visual hold, without extending interaction range or authorizing an action.

Buttons activate on release inside their original touch target. Crossing another button cannot transfer ownership. Owned drags are consumed; cancellation, resize, modal opening and focus loss clear pending touches immediately. The stick owns a separate movement finger. Emulated mouse events are suppressed for gameplay controls only; real mouse buttons and native menu controls remain available. World/SPELL still decides action eligibility and timing.

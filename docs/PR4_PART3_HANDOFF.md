# PR #4 Part 3 handoff

Part 2 presentation consumes `World.Presentation` through `CombatPresentationController`; retain this single observation stream. The world continues to own action timing, damage, targeting, dodge, projectiles, death and persistence. Do not add a second damage executor or retrigger PPC actions for `AttackCommitted`/`AttackImpact`.

`AbilityDef.ActionStyle` accepts the existing PPC enum. `slash` uses `Strike`, `bite` uses `Bite`, and the verification-only directional sweep uses `TailSwipe`. Omission uses the creature rig's default. TailSwipe/Sting, WingBuffet, TentacleLash and PincerSnap check the corresponding generated appendages and fall back to the rig action if absent. The motor maps the world's windup/recovery into `ActionPlayer.MatchCombat`; view synchronization supplies elapsed time from authoritative state so catch-up frames do not advance the pose ahead of impact. Cancellation clears pending and active actions. Restored windups seek to their elapsed point without another start event.

The controller maps `Hit`, `Miss`, `DefensiveAvoidance`, cancellation and death independently, including separate victim reactions for a cone. Use captured `Origin`/`Position` for contact and keep victim yaw stable. A same-tick matching avoidance suppresses its miss cue. Periodic damage with an empty ability ID is not a weapon action. A death follows its fatal hit and should not replay the hit. Dead views reconstruct their persistent anatomy-specific rest posture and loot marker without serializing particles or sound.

`ProjectileReleased` is a release, not a hit. `ProjectileImpact` must only be emitted by a real collision; existing projectiles can expire or disappear on terrain without that event. The controller briefly coalesces the collision with its immediately following `Hit` or `Miss` into one impact burst; avoidance suppresses that burst, and a collision with no follow-up is flushed after the world step. If Part 3 implements additional collision kinds, define and test their authoritative event semantics first; do not fake a terrain impact or duplicate existing release/collision effects.

`CombatEffects` holds 48 bursts, each with at most seven generated fragments and a lifetime at most 0.64 seconds; high-priority effects can replace lower-priority ones. It projects an event's ground position once and uses a stable event-derived hash, not world randomness. Eight cached audio voices, a two-per-kind limit, distance gating and priority replacement bound overlapping cues; a view reset stops transient voices while preserving ambience and music. Camera feedback is an additive, confined, decaying offset; `Strength = 0` disables it. Keep telegraphs legible above terrain and beneath important actor/action feedback.

Character views request a bounded 30 FPS PPC pose rate only during actions, brief hit reactions and the established biped dodge; ordinary poses retain their previous rates and offscreen culling. The existing pre-draw worker batch joins before upload. Profile several active enemies under the same rendered workload before changing these limits; use the five normal-time scenarios in `VisualChecks` and the production replay rather than Movie Maker wall-time as a frame-rate measure. Tactical AI, spacing, projectile movement, broad encounter balance and mobile controls remain Part 3/4 work.

Camera follow smoothing uses physics callbacks to avoid Godot's variable-frame extrapolation during severe hitches. Keep the additive `CameraFeedback` offset bounded and preserve the rendered repeated-hit hitch regression when adding new impact types.
